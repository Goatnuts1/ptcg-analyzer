# Meta scan — 2026-08-20

First run of the weekly meta-scan procedure (now `/meta-scan`). Sources: the live
Limitless PBL-Standard table, release-calendar sweep, the repo's own mcts2 matrix
(docs/matrix_2026-08_mcts2.json), and the ladder log.

## Meta drift since 2026-08-17 (3 days)

Effectively none — every top-10 share moved <0.4pt. The snapshot the overnight matrix
was built on is still current; no re-measurement needed. Notables at the margin:
- **Raging Bolt Ogerpon is the quiet riser**: 1.79→1.97% share at the format's #2 real
  win rate (53.87%). Watch it.
- Cynthia's Garchomp ticked up in WR (52.23→52.92%) — consistent with our ladder
  encounter (2026-08-19).
- The recent ladder opponents' decks are confirmed FRINGE: Mega Darkrai is rank 37 at
  39.10% WR; Mega Zygarde/Audino is outside the top 50. Ladder sees more off-meta than
  the tournament table implies — the deck's matchup spread matters more than its
  top-10 row.
