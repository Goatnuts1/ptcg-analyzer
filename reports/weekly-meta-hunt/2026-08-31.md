## f. Crustle Wall — Proposed Adjustments

**One swap made this week, grounded directly in the Worlds semifinal result:**

**Jumbo Ice Cream in (+1), Crushing Hammer out (-1, leaving 1 copy).**
Real card text (Phantasmal Flames #91, verified): *"Heal 80 damage from your Active
Pokémon that has 3 or more Energy attached."* Satoshi Matsui's semifinal-finishing Crustle
list ran **4 copies** as its primary sustain tool. It's a clean fit for this project's own
`crustle_modern` thesis — a wall deck's whole plan is staying alive, and 80 HP back onto a
3+-Energy wall (which, once Hero's Cape and the Mist/Spiky Energy package are attached, is
exactly what a fully-set-up Crustle or Milotic ex looks like) directly extends that plan.
It was **already implemented and tested** in this engine (`effects.py`,
`tests/test_jumbo_ice_cream.py`) but simply wasn't in this deck's 60. Crushing Hammer's
coin-flip energy denial is the softest, least-reliable card in the current 33 Trainers, and
notably **the real Worlds semifinal list doesn't run any Crushing Hammer at all** —
cutting one copy (not both, to keep some denial against a set-up-dependent opponent) to
fund the add is a minimal, defensible, single-card change.

**Why this is a swap and not a rebuild:** Matsui's real list is a much bigger departure
from `crustle_modern` than one card — it drops Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex and Milotic ex
entirely for 4x Mega Kangaskhan ex, and runs several cards this engine does **not** yet
implement (Xerosic's Machinations, Pokémon Center Lady, Handheld Fan, Lumiose City,
Growing Grass Energy — verified real, current-Standard cards, checked against
`effects.py` and found absent). Rebuilding around a still-partially-unbuildable list this
week would be exactly the kind of over-reaction the report should avoid. The other
already-implemented cards from his list not yet in `crustle_modern` (Team Rocket's Petrel,
Eri, Special Red Card, Enhanced Hammer, Team Rocket's Factory, Festival Grounds) are real
candidates for **future** individual swaps, not bundled into this one — see §g.
