## e. Ogerpon Box — Proposed Adjustments

**One swap made this week: Unfair Stamp (ACE SPEC) → Prime Catcher (ACE SPEC).**
Both cards were already fully implemented in `effects.py` before this session (no new
engine code needed) and the deck still runs exactly one ACE SPEC, so no legality rule
changes. Reasoning:
- Unfair Stamp's `can_play` is gated on "a Pokémon of yours was KO'd last turn" —
  reactive, and dead in hand on many turns.
- Prime Catcher (gust the opponent's Active out from the Bench + switch your own) is
  playable any turn the opponent has a Bench — much more consistently useful for
  setting up and closing out KOs.
- This is exactly the ACE SPEC Cali White ran in her real, verified 13th-place
  Worlds 2026 Ogerpon Box list (§b) — not a guess, a real pilot's choice at the
  highest level of play.
- Verified in-sim: both decks still load to 60 legal cards and play full games under
  greedy without error (`python3 cli.py --deck1 ogerpon_box --deck2 crustle_modern
  --games 20 --seed 42` ran clean post-edit).

**Not done this week, flagged as homework (§g):** Cali White's real list is a much
bigger structural departure from `ogerpon_box` than one card — it runs Mega
Kangaskhan ex as the 4-of headliner (ours runs Teal Mask Ogerpon ex at 4 and Kangaskhan
at 2), replaces most of the single-target energy-attach package with 4x Crispin
(search 2 different-typed Basic Energy, attach 1 + hand 1), adds Cyrano and Glass
Trumpet, and spreads across a 5-type energy base instead of our near-mono-Grass one.
Every one of those cards (Crispin, Cyrano, Mega Kangaskhan ex, Chien-Pao, Raging Bolt
ex, Bloodmoon Ursaluna ex) is **already implemented** in this engine. That's a real
rebuild candidate, not a single swap — deliberately not doing it blind this week.

## f. Crustle Wall — Proposed Adjustments

**One swap made this week: 1 of 2 Crushing Hammer → Enhanced Hammer.**
Both already implemented in `effects.py`, no new engine code. Reasoning:
- Rune Heiremans' real, verified 5th-place Worlds 2026 Crustle list (§b) ran **1
  Enhanced Hammer and zero Crushing Hammer.**
- Crushing Hammer is a coin-flip against any attached Energy; Enhanced Hammer is a
  guaranteed hit but Special-Energy-only. Given a meta where Dragapult ex itself runs
  Special Energy variants and our own Mist/Spiky Energy tech is the exact kind of
  card an opponent's Special-Energy hate would want to strip, a guaranteed
  Special-Energy Enhanced Hammer is a reasonable, evidence-backed trade for one of
  the two coin-flip copies. Kept 1 Crushing Hammer rather than going to 2x Enhanced
  Hammer since it still covers Basic Energy too, and one real decklist is one data
  point, not a mandate to fully copy it.
- Verified in-sim alongside the Ogerpon Box swap (see §e); `crustle_modern` still
  loads to 60 legal cards and plays clean.

**Bouffalant: downgrading, not implementing.** Last week's homework item #1 proposed
scripting Bouffalant's Curly Wall this session. New evidence this week argues against
prioritizing it: **it does not appear anywhere in Rune Heiremans' real, top-8 Worlds
2026 Crustle decklist** — the actual highest-level pilot playing this actual
archetype at this actual event chose Team Rocket's Petrel / Eri / Jumbo Ice Cream /
Festival Grounds / Team Rocket's Factory / Lumiose City instead. A single columnist's
pre-Worlds theorycraft not surviving contact with a real Worlds top-cut list is a
real (if soft) signal. Retiring this as an active lead rather than carrying it
forward as unfinished homework — see §g for what replaces it.

**Not done this week, flagged as homework (§g):** Heiremans' real list is again a
much bigger departure than one swap — it drops **both** of `crustle_modern`'s named
"second wall" pieces (Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex AND Milotic ex) entirely, in favor
of Team Rocket's Petrel (draw/search), Eri (Item lock/disruption), Jumbo Ice Cream
(healing), Xerosic's Machinations, Pokémon Center Lady, Special Red Card, Festival
Grounds, and Team Rocket's Factory. Every one of those is **already implemented**.
This directly contradicts this deck's own header-comment thesis in `decks.py`
("Milotic ex as a second wall... to push past exactly the weakness this project's
own real losses exposed") — worth a real discussion next session, not a silent
swap, since it changes the deck's whole identity.
