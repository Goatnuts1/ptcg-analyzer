## a. Deck Recap (One-Pager)

**Teal Mask Ogerpon Box** (`ogerpon_box` in `decks.py`, the "Basic Box"/"ogerpon clefairy"
toolbox shell — Teal Mask Ogerpon ex + Lillie's Clefairy ex core)
- Game plan: Grass-type toolbox built around Teal Mask Ogerpon ex's Teal Dance (free
  Grass energy acceleration + draw), backed by Lillie's Clefairy ex / Latias ex /
  Meowth ex / Pecharunt / Mega Kangaskhan ex / Munkidori / Fezandipiti ex / Chi-Yu /
  Wellspring Mask Ogerpon ex.

  one-Basic-type deck punch above its speed. Worlds Top 8 validated (2026-09-14), won
  the 3,122-player Baltimore Regional (2026-09-20), and this week it's confirmed still
  a healthy, winning archetype at the next Regional too (see §b).
- Biggest weakness: universal Grass Weakness ×2 to Fire is unchanged and structural.
- This week: **stable, confirmed continuity, no urgent changes.** "Basic Box" ("ogerpon
  clefairy" on Limitless — the exact pairing this repo's list is built around) went
  12th of 1,074 at the Recife Regional with a healthy 50.09% event win rate across a
  5.9%-of-field sample. Nothing new and alarming, nothing new and actionable either.

**Crustle Wall** (`crustle_modern` in `decks.py`)
- Game plan: stall behind Crustle's Mysterious Rock Inn Ability ("Prevent all damage
  done to this Pokémon by attacks from your opponent's Pokémon **ex**"), doubled up
  with Milotic ex (Sparkling Scales) and Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex; Hero's Cape /
  Mist Energy / Spiky Energy / Crushing Hammer round out the tech.
- Biggest strength: this repo's own Aug 20 meta-scan still has it as the only
  20-candidate deck with zero losing matchups (floor 53.3%), and it made Worlds Top 8.
- Biggest weakness: Mysterious Rock Inn is gated on the attacker being Pokémon-**ex**
  — a non-ex attacker walks past it regardless of how it deals damage. Last week's
  report identified the first real-world deck to punish this (Alakazam/Dusknoir,
  Frankfurt Regional champion) — **that remains the single most important open
  thread for this deck**; nothing this week changes or resolves it either way.
- This week: **quiet on the Crustle side specifically.** No new Crustle finish could
  be confirmed at either Regional in this week's window (Recife, Louisville) — same
  "present but outside Top 32" pattern as the last several events, this time with no
  placement number even confirmable. No new tech surfaced that answers last week's
  Alakazam/Dusknoir finding.

## e. Ogerpon Box — Proposed Adjustments

**No changes warranted this week.** The one real finding (Recife's "Basic Box"
showing — §b) is reassuring continuity, not a reason to change anything: a 50.09%
event win rate across 63 pilots at a real Regional is a healthy, stable result for
an already-registered archetype, not a signal the list is falling behind.

The week's one serious swap candidate was evaluated and rejected: the Recife-winning
Ogerpon/Meganium list runs **Lana's Aid** (Twilight Masquerade #155 — "Return up to 3
in any combination of Pokémon (non Rule Box) and Basic Energy cards from your discard
pile to your hand"). It's tempting as a free-floating recovery Supporter, but it
doesn't fit this list on its own terms: our registered `ogerpon_box` is almost entirely
Rule-Box Pokémon (Teal Mask Ogerpon ex, Lillie's Clefairy ex, Latias ex, Meowth ex,
Mega Kangaskhan ex, Fezandipiti ex, Wellspring Mask Ogerpon ex are all **ex**, and
therefore ineligible) — only Pecharunt (2), Munkidori (1), and Chi-Yu (1) would
qualify, and our only Basic Energy is Grass (Prism Energy is Special Energy, also
ineligible). Four eligible Pokémon and one eligible Energy type is too thin a target
to earn a Supporter slot over Boss's Orders/N's Plan. **Not recommending it.** Dawn
and Briar (also in the winning list) are both evolution-engine support cards with no
target in a deck that runs zero Stage 1/2 Pokémon — not applicable at all.

No new counter-archetype with a confirmed, type-relevant attack line surfaced against
Grass/Fire Weakness this week, and no forecast item clears the relevance bar (§d).
