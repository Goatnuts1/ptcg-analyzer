## f. Crustle Wall — Proposed Adjustments

**No changes warranted this week — but this is the week's real finding, and it is
squarely about this deck.** Alakazam/Dusknoir just won a 2,793-player Regional
(§b), and this week's research pinned down exactly *why* it's a structural threat to
Crustle's whole wall plan, with real card text on both sides:

- **Crustle's Mysterious Rock Inn, confirmed exact text:** "Prevent all damage done
  to this Pokémon by attacks from your opponent's Pokémon **ex**." The gate is
  entirely on the *attacker's card having the ex subtype* — not on how the damage is
  dealt.
- **Alakazam, confirmed non-ex** (Stage 2, 140 HP, Psychic Draw / Powerful Hand:
  "Place 2 damage counters on your opponent's Active Pokémon for each card in your
  hand"). Because it is non-ex, Mysterious Rock Inn's ex-gate never engages against
  it at all — this matches the engine's own implementation (`_source_is_opp_ex` in
  `effects.py` checks the attacking card's subtypes, and Alakazam simply never
  qualifies), so this is not a simulator quirk, it is the real rule working exactly
  as printed.

This is the first time this project's own long-documented "non-ex chip damage is
Crustle's theoretical hole" note (`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/META_GAUNTLET_2026-08.md`) has
a real, named, Regional-winning deck behind it rather than a hypothetical. **No
decklist edit is proposed this week** because nothing found so far is a *specific
Crustle-side tech card* that answers Powerful Hand (it's a counter-based, not an
attack-damage-based, threat — Hero's Cape's flat +100 HP and Mist Energy's
attack-effect block are the closest existing tech in the list, and neither targets
"opponent's hand size" or "damage counters already placed" directly). This is
flagged as the top priority for next session's actual sim work (§g item 2), not as
something to edit blind.

One correction to log: last week's in-repo check of `crustle_modern` vs
`alakazam_deck` (93.3% greedy, n=150) used this repo's registered Alakazam/
**Dudunsparce** build, not Alakazam/**Dusknoir** — a different partner Pokémon and
likely a different Trainer line. That sim result says nothing definitive about the
