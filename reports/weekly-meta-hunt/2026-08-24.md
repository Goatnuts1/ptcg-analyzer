# Weekly Meta Hunt — 2026-08-24

## a. Deck Recap (One-Pager)

**Teal Mask Ogerpon Box** (`ogerpon_box` in `decks.py`)
- Game plan: Grass-type box built around Teal Mask Ogerpon ex's Teal Dance (free Grass
  energy acceleration), backed by Lillie's Clefairy ex / Latias ex / Meowth ex / Pecharunt
  / Mega Kangaskhan ex / Munkidori / Fezandipiti ex / Chi-Yu / Wellspring Mask Ogerpon ex.
- Biggest strength: energy acceleration lets a one-Basic-type deck punch above its speed.
- Biggest weakness: universal Grass Weakness ×2 to Fire — a real Fire matchup is a bad
  day by default; the repo's own rotation-exposure model also flags it as the higher-risk
  of the two decks (38.3% of its list is mark-H, vs. Crustle's 21.7%).
- This week: **no repo-side change** — the deck is unblocked (present on `main` since last
  week) but no new evidence surfaced strong enough to justify touching the list. See §e.

**Crustle Wall** (`crustle_modern` in `decks.py` — the tech-updated build; see §f for lineage)
- Game plan: stall behind Crustle's Mysterious Rock Inn Ability (blocks all damage from
  opposing Pokémon-**ex** attacks), doubled up with Milotic ex (Sparkling Scales, a second
  wall) and Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex; Hero's Cape / Mist Energy / Spiky Energy / Crushing
  Hammer round out the tech against exactly the "non-ex chip damage" hole this shell is
  known for.
- Biggest strength: per this repo's own Aug 20 meta-scan, `crustle_modern` is the top or
  co-top **stopper** against every single high-win-rate live threat measured (Dragapult
  86.7%, Raging Bolt Ogerpon 88.3%, Alakazam 78.3%, Grimmsnarl Froslass 81.7%) and is the
  **only** deck in the whole 20-candidate registry with no losing matchup at all
  (`docs/BEST_DECK_2026-08.md`, floor 53.3%).
- Biggest weakness: Mysterious Rock Inn only blocks **ex**-attack damage — a non-ex chip
  attacker, or an opposing Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex's Demolish (which explicitly ignores
  effects on the opponent's Active — i.e. bypasses the wall by card text, not a rules
  workaround), walks straight through. Confirmed this week: **the engine already models
  this correctly** (`effects.py` — Demolish is in `ATTACK_EFFECT_OWNS_DAMAGE` precisely to
  bypass Mysterious Rock Inn / Cornerstone Stance / Sparkling Scales). No engine gap here.
- This week: one real, verified-card lead surfaced (Bouffalant, SCR 119) but not
  strong/confirmed enough to act on yet — see §f and §g.

## f. Crustle Wall — Proposed Adjustments

**No changes warranted this week — but one lead worth a follow-up before next week's pass.**
Bouffalant (SCR 119) is a real, verified card that PokeBeach names as flipping Crustle's
Slowking/Festival Lead/Hide 'n Sneak matchups. I'm not proposing it as a swap yet, for two
concrete reasons:
1. **Its Ability only helps a narrow slice of this list.** Curly Wall reduces damage to
   Basic **Colorless** Pokémon only, and needs 2 copies in play simultaneously to activate
   at all. Of `crustle_modern`'s attackers, that's Mega Kangaskhan ex — not Dwebble/Crustle
   (the actual wall) or Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex. The PokeBeach framing ("flips the
   matchup") may be accurate, but the mechanism doesn't obviously match "protects the wall
   itself," so I'd rather sim-test it than guess.
2. **It isn't implemented.** Bouffalant has no entry in `effects.py` and isn't in the card
   pool yet. Per this project's own rule (CLAUDE.md: every model output validated against
   the engine before it's trusted), adding it to a decklist without a scripted Ability
   would silently make it a vanilla 130-damage attacker in-sim — which would neither
   confirm nor refute the real-world claim, just misrepresent it.

This is a good candidate for next session's actual engine work (see §g), not a blind
decklist edit this week.
