  two weekly reports) that Festival Lead's non-ex attackers are Crustle's real
  counter-play. Two honest readings, not a decklist finding either way: (1) this repo's

  or (2) the "Festival Lead beats Crustle" framing in circulation was always a
  mechanism-level claim ("non-ex damage walks past Mysterious Rock Inn") rather than a

  Flagging both readings rather than picking one — see §g for the concrete follow-up.
