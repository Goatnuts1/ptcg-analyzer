MATCHUP WATCH
Pittsburgh and Baltimore Regionals both wrapped this weekend, but no confirmed Standard decklists made it out yet — too fresh, and a search even mixed up the Pittsburgh TCG results with the video-game bracket (flagged and thrown out, not reported). So instead of officials, this week's finding came from the sim itself: we finally ran crustle_modern vs. festival_lead, the exact matchup community chatter keeps naming as Crustle's real answer (non-ex damage walks past Mysterious Rock Inn). The engine says otherwise — Crustle won 82.5% under greedy (n=200) and 85% with both sides on mcts2 (n=40). Either Festival Lead is still being mispiloted in this engine, or the "Festival Lead beats Crustle" line was never a real head-to-head number to begin with. Also: re-checked Bouffalant's Curly Wall text, and it shields all your Basic Pokémon, not just Colorless ones — wider than last month's note said.
