OFFICIALS
World Championships 2026 ran Aug 28–30 in San Francisco. Andrew Hedrick (USA) won it all with Dragapult ex, 13–2–0, beating Diego Cassiraga in the final. But the story for this project: Satoshi Matsui piloted Crustle to the semifinal — the same wall archetype behind crustle_modern — before losing 2–0 to the eventual champion. Ogerpon Box also made real Worlds top cut (~15% of the field), though sources disagree on the exact pilot/placement this week — flagged honestly in the report rather than guessed at.
TECH TIP / SWAP MADE
Matsui's verified semifinal decklist ran 4x Jumbo Ice Cream (real text: heal 80 from your Active with 3+ Energy attached) as its sustain plan — already implemented in this engine but missing from crustle_modern. One copy is in this week, funded by trimming Crushing Hammer's unreliable coin-flip denial from 2 copies to 1. A single, low-risk, evidence-backed card swap — not a rebuild toward his much bigger list (which also drops Cornerstone Ogerpon ex and Milotic ex for 4x Mega Kangaskhan ex).
FORECAST / MATCHUP WATCH
Delta Reign (Nov 6) revealed Mega Golisopod ex's real attack text: Punishing Needle, 10+50 damage per opposing Pokémon with an Ability — a pointed threat shape against an Ability-stacked wall like Crustle. But Golisopod ex is itself a Pokémon-ex, and Mysterious Rock Inn already blocks all opposing ex-attack damage while Crustle is Active — on the text alone this reads like a hole the wall already covers, but that's a reading, not a confirmed sim result. Homework item, not a fix needed yet.
